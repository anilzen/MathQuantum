Strategize your entry into the QIS workforce by gaining insight into what employers are looking for.

Come learn which jobs, skills, and degrees are currently relevant in quantum information science.

You will actively explore data related related to quantum information science industry needs and job postings in the field. Participants will co-summarize and discuss with other attendees.

The focus will be on the industry sector, but this is a participant-driven workshop so you optionally can explore other sectors of interest.

_Please bring a laptop for full participation_
